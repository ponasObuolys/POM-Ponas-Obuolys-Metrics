# POM 2.4.2: Fable/Mythos and Grok Bot in History, tidier charts

**Download `POM-2.4.2.dmg` below. Requires macOS 14+; Apple silicon and Intel.**

- **Fable and Mythos tokens on their own.** History shows how much of the Claude total was used by Fable and Mythos models: a dotted line in the chart and a line in the Claude card with its share. It is a part of the Claude total, not an addition.
- **Grok Bot history.** With **Grok Bot from server** enabled, a new card and bar chart show the share of the weekly limit used each day. Grok Bot does not report tokens for personal plans, so this is a percentage and has its own chart. The server reports the running week only; POM keeps earlier weeks on this Mac for 30 days.
- **Chart dates line up.** Date labels were shifted to the right of their points and today's label was missing. Each label now sits under its point, and the cards in a row share one height.
- **No more permanent "some Claude records could not be read".** The count of unreadable records never expired, so one bad line hid the average and week comparison forever. It is now kept per session file and ends when that file is rewritten or leaves the 30-day window.

After the update POM reads the last 30 days of Claude sessions once more, because the old index did not store the model. On a Mac with several gigabytes of sessions this takes some minutes in small background batches; totals are marked partial until it finishes.

## Validation

489 core checks and the SwiftUI rendering/integration checks passed, including the model split, an index upgraded from 2.4.1, the per-file warning, Grok Bot response parsing, two weekly periods with a shared reset day, opt-in, a failed refresh and an account switch.

Live checks on 2026-11-25: a full import of 9 GB of real Claude sessions reproduced the 2.4.1 daily totals, reported no unreadable records and attributed 743 of 1,279 million tokens in the last 7 days to Fable and Mythos. One read-only request to Grok Bot's server returned six daily shares totalling 84.5% of the weekly limit. The Grok Bot history fetch inside the app was exercised with injected readings, not with the live account.
