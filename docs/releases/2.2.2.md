# POM 2.2.2 — support POM

**Download `POM-2.2.2.dmg` below. Requires macOS 14+; supports Apple silicon and Intel.**

- A heart button in the usage window and **Support POM…** in Settings open a voluntary, one-time Stripe support page.
- Choose your own amount in EUR; €5 is suggested. Support goes to Ponas Obuolys, MB. POM remains free and open source, with no subscription or paid features.
- The app opens your browser only when you click. No payment SDK, background requests or tracking were added. POM does not receive your payment details.
- English and Lithuanian labels, support page copy and confirmation message are included. The repository now also has a Sponsor link.

**Not Apple-notarized:** this release uses an ad-hoc app signature. macOS may block the first launch; it does not resolve the warning seen with earlier POM releases. See [Apple’s explanation](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445). The SHA-256 checksum and signed update feed verify integrity, not Apple approval.

Quit POM, open the DMG and replace POM in Applications. The signed appcast directs existing users to this release page for manual installation.

Validation: LT/EN SwiftUI renders in light and dark appearances; universal arm64/x86_64 compilation; strict app signature, DMG checksum and signed appcast verification. The mounted DMG executable matches the locally installed 2.2.2 app. The live support page was checked for recipient, description and suggested amount; no payment was made.

**Lietuviškai:** lango apačioje pridėta širdelė ir nustatymų meniu punktas **Paremti POM…**. Jie atveria vienkartinės Stripe paramos puslapį. Sumą pasirenkate patys, siūlomi 5 €. Gavėjas – Ponas Obuolys, MB. Programa lieka nemokama; papildomų foninių procesų nėra. Leidimas nenotarizuotas.
